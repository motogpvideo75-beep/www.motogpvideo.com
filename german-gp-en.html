LIVE RACES

Fox Sports — FS1 / FS2

Fox Sports is the home of MotoGP in the US: every Grand Prix and every sprint race airs on FS1 or FS2 and streams on the Fox Sports app. Cord-cutters can get FS1/FS2 through live-TV services such as Fubo or DIRECTV.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does the German GP start in the USA?

The Grand Prix starts Sunday, July 12, 2026 at 8:00 AM ET (7:00 AM CT / 5:00 AM PT). The sprint runs Saturday at 9:00 AM ET.

What channel is MotoGP on in the US?

Fox Sports: every Grand Prix and sprint airs on FS1 or FS2, with streaming on the Fox Sports app and via live-TV services that carry those channels.

Can I watch anything for free?

Yes — the free MotoGP Channel (on Prime Video, FireTV, LG Channels, Fubo, Plex, Sling Freestream) carries practice, qualifying and live Moto2/Moto3/MotoE. The premier-class races themselves air on FS1/FS2.

Are unofficial streaming sites safe?

We strongly advise against them — they are illegal and frequently loaded with malware and scam ads. Between Fox Sports, the free MotoGP Channel and the official VideoPass, there are plenty of legal options.
