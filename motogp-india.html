MotoGP India — Bharat GP Return

Is MotoGP coming back to the Buddh International Circuit? Everything we know about the Bharat GP: what happened, the new government deal, the 2027 outlook — and how to watch MotoGP legally in India right now.

● Current Status

Not on the 2026 calendar — 2027 return under negotiation after a new agreement between the Uttar Pradesh Government and AVW Global.
  • 2023 — First Bharat GP ✔

    MotoGP raced at the Buddh International Circuit for the first time. Despite extreme heat and humidity, fan turnout was strong and the weekend was seen as a breakthrough for Indian motorsport.

  • 2024 — Cancelled

    The race was cancelled after the local promoter failed to pay the hosting fee. Dorna cut ties with the promoter and signed a direct three-year agreement with the Government of Uttar Pradesh.

  • 2025–2026 — Postponed, then dropped

    The rescheduled 2025 race was pushed to 2026 citing operational circumstances, and India was then left off the 2026 calendar entirely.

  • Feb 2026 — New agreement, 2027 target

    The Uttar Pradesh Government signed an MoU with AVW Global, a motorsport events and advisory company, in a renewed push to bring international motorcycle racing — including MotoGP and the Asia Road Racing Championship — back to Buddh by 2027.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Will MotoGP return to India?

It is not on the 2026 calendar, but the new UP Government–AVW Global agreement targets a return of international motorcycle racing to Buddh by 2027. No date is confirmed yet — bookmark this page, we update it as news breaks.

Why was the Bharat GP cancelled?

The 2024 edition was cancelled over an unpaid hosting fee by the original promoter. Later editions fell through due to scheduling and operational issues, despite a direct deal between Dorna and the state government.

Is MotoGP free to watch in India?

Eurosport India is included in many TV packages and JioTV offers access on mobile. FanCode and VideoPass are paid streaming options. Some official highlights are free on MotoGP's YouTube channel.

Could Formula 1 also return to India?

An F1 comeback looks unlikely in the short term due to the packed F1 calendar, though a rotational slot has been discussed. MotoGP remains the most realistic international series to return to Buddh first.
